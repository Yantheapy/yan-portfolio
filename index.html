Capital raise · cultural institution, Dubai

A landmark private collection, made investable

Byzantine & Eastern-Christian heritage museum, Dubai · leading the investment case and capital raise · 2025–present
Challenge

Turn a landmark private collection into a board-ready vehicle a family office or strategic investor could underwrite — an institution, not a passion project.

What I did

The full investor package: multi-year financial model (CAPEX, financing, monthly P&L and cash flow, IRR / NPV, scenarios, Monte-Carlo), a ~70-page business plan, visitor and market analysis benchmarked to Louvre Abu Dhabi and the Met, and the equity and governance structure for the raise.

An AED 1.0B+ collection structured into a governed, capitalised institution with a AED 204.8M project-finance facility — positioned as cultural diplomacy aligned with the UAE cultural-tourism strategy and the UN SDGs. Capital raise active.

Operational transformation · DHA-regulated clinic, Dubai

From scattered documents to an audit-ready clinic

Premium dental clinic, Dubai · transformation mandate · 2026
Challenge

Scaling a regulated clinic while governance, quality, sales and operations lived in scattered documents — no single, access-controlled source of truth and no way to see the numbers.

What I did

Mapped and redesigned the core business processes, built the governance and quality backbone — a 31-KPI Balanced Scorecard, 29 SOPs, CAPA continuous improvement, patient-risk and RAID registers — and put it on one internal platform for management and clinicians, bilingual RU/EN.

Result

Governance, marketing and finance model in place in seven weeks; the scorecard live; operations measurable and audit-ready, with the clinic on a JCI-accreditation pathway.
